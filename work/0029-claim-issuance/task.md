# Task: Issue one system-managed SandboxClaim

- Ticket: [#29](https://github.com/wunderforge/agenova/issues/29)
- Mission: Turn one admitted request and its resolved EffectiveAuthority into exactly one system-issued `Pending` claim snapshot that a caller can neither supply nor later alter.
- Target: a new `internal/issuance/` package, the shared `SandboxClaim`/`IssuedState` types from #25, focused tests, and this task packet.
- User value: an authorized assignment becomes one inspectable, system-issued record of who was granted what, while a denied request leaves no claim behind.
- PRD outcome: [Claim lifecycle](../../docs/product/prd.md#2-claim-lifecycle) and [Claim-scoped authority](../../docs/product/prd.md#4-claim-scoped-authority)

## Scope

In scope:

- Add a pure issuance step that consumes the #27 Gate-issued admission, the validated ClaimRequest, the trusted Principal, the emitted Decision, and the #28-resolved EffectiveAuthority.
- Generate the system-managed claim identity and the system-managed authority identity that #28 deliberately left unset.
- Emit one `Pending` claim with no `backendIdentity`, correlated `requestRef`, `templateRef`, and `authorityRef`.
- Assemble the complete Allow-form `IssuedState` (principal, action, policy reference, authority, claim, decision, evidence) and validate it before returning.
- Copy the resolved authority into the snapshot so later mutation of the request, template, policy, or authority inputs cannot change an issued claim.
- Make repeated issuance of the same admitted request deterministic by content-addressed derivation, following the `decision:` identity convention #27 already established.
- Produce no claim for `Deny` or `ApprovalRequired`, and no partially issued state when validation fails.
- Prove issuance, rejection, determinism, and snapshot immutability against the canonical Team A and Team B fixtures plus table-driven cases.

Out of scope:

- Deciding admission (#27) or computing the authority intersection (#28).
- Backend allocation, worker binding, `backendIdentity` population, or any phase transition past `Pending` (#30, #31).
- Composing issuance into the run service, CLI, or console (#31, #41, #60).
- Governed Tool or Model invocation enforcement (#34, #35).
- Credential issuance, evidence persistence, a claim store, distributed transactions, or production idempotency storage.
- Adding claim fields or lifecycle semantics beyond the shape owned by #25.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Issuance is impossible without a #27 Gate-issued admission bound to this exact request, project, and template; a public or replayed `Allow` decision cannot produce a claim.
- The canonical Team A admitted request produces exactly one claim: `phase` is `Pending`, `backendIdentity` is absent, and the returned `IssuedState` passes `ValidateIssuedState`.
- `claim.id` and `effectiveAuthority.id` are generated by Agenova; no caller-supplied value reaches either field, and `ParseCallerIssuedState` still rejects a payload carrying them.
- `claim.requestRef` matches the request, `claim.templateRef` matches `action.templateRef`, `claim.authorityRef` matches `effectiveAuthority.id`, and `evidence.claimId` matches `claim.id`.
- The issued authority equals the #28 resolution plus the assigned identity; issuance never recomputes, reorders, or enlarges any authority dimension.
- Issuing twice from identical admitted inputs yields byte-identical snapshots, and any change to the admitted inputs yields a different claim identity.
- A `Deny` or `ApprovalRequired` decision, a missing authority, or a failed invariant produces an explicit error and no claim, no authority identity, and no partially populated issued state.
- Mutating the source request, template, policy, or resolved authority after issuance cannot change the issued snapshot.
- The issuance step stays pure and backend-neutral: no backend vocabulary, no clock or random source in the identity path, and no I/O.

## Negative Case

- Given a Team B request that the Gate denies, when issuance is attempted, then no claim and no authority identity are produced and only the existing denial evidence path remains observable.

## Decisions and Blockers

- Planning depth: Task + Spec because the issued claim snapshot is a shared contract consumed by the run service, gateways, CLI, and console; no separate Design is needed because the Ticket's non-goal excludes idempotency storage and leaves one pure approach.
- Proposed decision: issuance is a pure function over admitted inputs, so "deterministic repeated issuance" means identical inputs reproduce an identical snapshot rather than a stored idempotency record.
- Proposed decision: derive `claim.id` and `effectiveAuthority.id` by content-addressed sha256 over the admitted request, principal, decision, and resolved authority, formatted as `claim:<requestRef>:issuance:<hex>` and `authority:<requestRef>:issuance:<hex>`, extending the `decision:<requestRef>:authorization:<hex>` convention #27 already established.
- Proposed decision: issuance returns the complete Allow-form `IssuedState` rather than a bare `SandboxClaim`, because `ValidateIssuedState` already owns the cross-object invariants this Ticket must satisfy.
- Proposed decision: new package `internal/issuance`, parallel to `internal/authorization` (#27) and `internal/authority` (#28).
- Open decision for the Owner: the shared fixture uses the illustrative identity `claim:fix-payment-timeout:1`. Confirm that issuance follows the #27 content-addressed convention and that the fixture remains a shape oracle rather than a literal identity oracle.
- Open decision for the Owner: confirm that `evidence.runtimeEvents` is empty at issuance, because the canonical fixture's `ClaimRunning` event belongs to the later `Running` transition owned by #30/#31.
- Resolved dependencies: #27 and #28 both merged to `main`; #28's Ticket is still open only because PR #97 merged through the stacked branch rather than directly.
